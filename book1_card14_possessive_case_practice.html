Use the Possessive Case

a) The computer of the girl.
b) The computer of the girls.
c) The bedroom of Elisa and Martha.
d) The bedroom of Paul and Mathias.
e) The name of my mother is Lea.
f) The names of my teachers are Carolina and Evandro.
g) The names of the boys are Andrew and James.
h) The car of the boy is a Ferrari.
i) The computers of the girls are broken (quebrado).
