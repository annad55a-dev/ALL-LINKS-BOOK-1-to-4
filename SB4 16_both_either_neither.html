Exercise – Complete With: “Both”, “Either” Or “Neither”

Remember: Both = this AND that. Either = this OR that. Neither = not this and not that (use with a positive verb).

1. “Where would you like to eat: the Chinese or the Italian restaurant?” I don't like eating out so I don't really want to go to of them.

2. I love my parents the same.

3. me nor my husband like drinking beer.

4. I don't know which blouse I prefer! Can I have of them, dad?

5. Could you or Pedro give me a pen, please!

6. of them want to go to the cinema with me so I will go with another friend.

7. I am rich enough this year to go to Canada and England!

8. I would like meat or chicken for dinner.

9. I'm not hungry. I want meat nor chicken for dinner.

10. I know Russian nor Chinese. I want to learn these languages.

11. of us liked the party. It was fantastic!

12. of us liked the party. It was really boring!

13. She speak Russian nor Italian.

14. I like them .

15. Would you like tea or coffee? of them; I don't mind.

16. of them studied for the exam. They didn't pass.

Item 10's second blank is a free-response space and is not graded (the sentence works with just "neither...nor").
