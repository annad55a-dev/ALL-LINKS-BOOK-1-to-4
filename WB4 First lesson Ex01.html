First Lesson - Exercise 01

1.Is this book too difficult for you?
2.Are my (trousers/skirt/jeans) too (long/short) for you?
3.Are my shoes too large for you?
4.Is this room too big for a hundred (100) chairs?
5.Is the window too high for you to reach?
6.Is it too cold for you to go out?
7.Is Paris too far for you to go for the weekend?
8.Is a lorry too large for people to drive around the town?
9.Are you rich enough to buy a large house in New York?
10.Is she tall enough to change the light bulb without standing on the chair?
11.Is Paul McCartney rich enough to buy an airplane?
12.Are you rich enough to buy Buckingham Palace?
13.Can you run fast enough to catch Usain Bolt?
14.Have you got enough money in your pocket?
15.Is there usually enough food in your fridge?
16.What do you do when you don't have enough money to travel?
17.What do you do when you don't have enough time to do the shopping?
18.Do many people in poor countries have enough money?
19.Have you got enough money to travel around the world?
20.Is there enough money in your pocket for you to buy a Ferrari?
21.Have you got enough time to travel to the beach next weekend?
22.Have you got enough time to come here every day?
23.Are there enough chairs in this room for everybody to sit?
24.Were there enough chairs in this room for everybody to sit yesterday?
25.Is there enough money in your bank account for you to buy an airplane?
