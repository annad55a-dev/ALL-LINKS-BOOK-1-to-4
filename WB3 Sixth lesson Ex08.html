Sixth Lesson - Exercise 08

Make sentences using the comparatives: "AS … AS", or "NOT AS … AS".
1.The blue car is the red one. (fast)
2.John is Hannah. (not/tall)
3.The guitar the piano. (not heavy)
4.This book is the other one. (good)
5.Nathan is Jeremy. (not fat)
6.Margaret her sister. (beautiful)
7.A Fusca a Ferrari. (not/expensive)
8.The moon the sun. (not/far)
9.This film that film. (bad)
10.An airplane as a truck. (not/slow)
